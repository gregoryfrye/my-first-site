## Situation

In spring 2022, Hudson Hemp — a family-run regenerative farm and CBD business in Hudson, NY — secured one of New York's first adult-use cultivation licenses, ahead of a market that hadn't yet opened.

## Complication

Getting out of hemp debt meant redefining the entire operation for a market with heavy regulation, no playbook, and well-funded competitors moving fast. Craft credibility and commercial scale usually pull in opposite directions.

## Ask

After trimming to a core farm crew, the CEO brought me in as the first outside hire: pivot the CBD brand, Treaty, into a THC preroll brand, and build the creative function across brand, packaging, and marketing.

## Approach

I pitched something larger: launch Hudson Cannabis — a transparent, integrated farm with a recognizable house brand, quality strong enough to win discerning customers, and an operation innovative enough to attract out-of-state brands as scaling partners. The bet: if we were who we said we were — regenerative roots, healed soil — the product would sell itself.

## Outcome

The Hudson Cannabis house brand scaled past $500K/month — outselling the established multi-state partner brands we brought to New York — with category-leading products in prerolls and concentrates. Acquired December 2025.
