Federal illegality blocks most conventional marketing. Paid promotion is unavailable, posts get auto-flagged for the product itself, and accounts disappear. The person behind the counter is the channel.

So we built that channel. I pushed for and developed a budtender education program: our sales team trained every new dispensary that took on the product and returned seasonally with new strains, vocabulary, and process methods. We hosted 5–10 farm tours a year, up to 50 guests each — roughly a thousand budtenders over three years, many of whom had never been to a farm, let alone a cannabis farm.
